# What problem I am solving?

As a multidisciplinary creator and Software/AI Engineer, I find existing social media platforms inadequate for meaningful content distribution, as they predominantly emphasize superficial entertainment over authentic storytelling. Leveraging my technical expertise to build a custom solution, I seek to develop an independent, minimalist platform to publish my creative works, media, and short-form vlogs with interactive commentary capabilities. This platform will simultaneously serve as a targeted professional showcase, providing industry peers and potential employers with direct insight into my technical capabilities, project portfolio, and professional identity.
