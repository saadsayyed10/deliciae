# Viewer Stories

## 2.1 - Profile Syncing

1. As a **Viewer**, I would like to be seen to the **Author**, so that I am allowed to access selected contents as well.
2. I would like to enter my **first and last name** for registration.
3. I would like to have my own **uniquely generated username**, so that I can later use it to **login back to the app**.
4. I **do not want password**, as my **username will be unique** and **available only to me and not even the Author**.
5. Another viewer and author **are not allowed** to access this feature.

## 2.2 - Viewing Contents of the Author

1. As a **Viewer**, I want to see all the released contents of the **Author**. 
2. I would prefer to have all the contents **sorted** into a **sidebar** as a **list** with their **children pages nested**.
3. I want to see **icon and title** of the **parent page** first and a **chevron down** icon that will **open and close** it’s **children pages** if they exist.
4. Once I click the **title** of the page, I want to go **inside of the page** to view all the contents.
5. Once I see the contents of the page, I want a **bothering comment on this post mini dialog** to the **right bottom of the screen** that will only **open** once it’s been **triggered by me**.
6. Author **is not allowed** to access this feature.

## 2.3 - Commenting on a post

1. As a **Viewer**, I would like to share my **opinion and thoughts** on the **Author’s** content post.
2. I want a text area where I can write a comment to the post that are available to me.
3. Author **is not allowed** to access this feature.

## 2.4 - Settings (Profile Management)

1. I want a dedicated page related to my information, where I can see the following:
    1. My Name, Status whether I am banned or not.
    2. Setting up profile picture.
    3. My comments collection.
    4. Messages from **Author**.
    5. And much more…
    6. Updating my account details.
    7. Deleting my account.
2. Author **is not allowed** to access this feature.
